````bash
$ curl -v -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"documentNumber\": \"47148589\", \"documentType\": \"DNI\", \"firstName\": \"Liliana\", \"lastName\": \"Pérez\", \"email\": \"liliana@gmail.com\", \"phone\": \"943525256\"}" http://localhost:8080/api/v1/customers | jq

< Transfer-Encoding: chunked
